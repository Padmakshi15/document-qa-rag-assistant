Document Retention Policy
=========================

Purpose
-------
This policy states how long uploaded documents are kept, who can access
them, and how they are deleted. It applies to every document indexed by
the Document Q&A Assistant.

Retention period
----------------
Uploaded documents and their indexed chunks are retained for 90 days
from the date of upload. After 90 days the document and its chunks are
automatically deleted from the vector store. A document owner may request
earlier deletion at any time, and deletion is completed within 5 business
days of the request.

Access
------
Only the person who uploaded a document can ask questions about it.
Uploaded documents are never shared with other users, and they are never
used to train any model. Access logs record the date of each upload and
each deletion, and nothing else.

Deletion
--------
When a document is deleted, its text, its chunks, and its embeddings are
all removed from the ChromaDB vector store. Deletion is permanent and
cannot be undone, so keep a copy of any document you may need later.
Backups, if any exist, are deleted within 30 days of the original
deletion.

Review
------
This policy is reviewed every 12 months. The next review is due 12
months after the last review date recorded in the policy register.
Questions about this policy should be sent to the document owner.
